About AJEEH

Building a reliable business around scrap metal.

AJEEH GENERAL ENTERPRISE LTD. is involved in the sourcing, buying, selling, supplying and distribution of iron scraps, scrap metals and non-ferrous materials.

We work with scrap collectors, individuals, businesses and other sources, buying materials and supplying them to buyers who need them.

Ferrous

Iron, steel and other ferrous scrap materials

Non-Ferrous

Our Services

What We Do

We handle the sourcing, collection, preparation and movement of scrap materials from different sources to their next destination.

Scrap Collection

We collect scrap materials from businesses, work sites, renovation projects and other locations where unwanted metal and reusable materials are available.

Sourcing & Supply

We source and supply iron scraps, scrap metals and non-ferrous materials for buyers looking for materials in different quantities.

Sorting & Processing

We sort and prepare collected materials for resale, recycling or further processing, depending on the type and condition of the material.

Transportation

We arrange the collection and transportation of materials from suppliers and sites to our yard or the required destination.
